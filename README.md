## GamePilot Nexus

**An AI that plays your PC games with you.** Pick a game window and Nexus can coach you or take the controls. It runs on your own Windows PC, so after a one time download it works offline. Real control starts off and STOP always wins.

Say "Hey Nexus" mid game and your companion answers out loud. It can point at the upgrade worth buying or the way out of a menu. It never clicks for you.

Early testers are playing now.
